What Play Console's Grow users → Store presence → Main store listing asks
for. Graphics are next to this file: `icon.png` (512x512) and
`feature-graphic.png` (1024x500), rendered from the SVGs beside them
(docs/building.md, "Android releases (Google Play)"). Phone screenshots
(2 to 8, 16:9 or 9:16, at least 1080 px on the short side for promotion) still
have to be taken on a device or the emulator.

## App name (30 characters at most)

```
Compass Browser
```

## Short description (80 characters at most)

```
Spaces, end-to-end encrypted sync and Claude, in a browser built on Chromium.
```

## Full description (4000 characters at most)

```
Compass is a browser that keeps your tabs in order. It's the Compass you may use on your Mac, Windows PC or iPhone, now on Android, and it's built on Chromium, so websites work as they do in Chrome.

SPACES
• Keep work, home and projects apart: each space has its own pinned tabs, folders and colors.
• Pin the tabs you come back to. Favorites stay at the top of every space.
• Today holds the tabs you open along the way, and tidies them into the archive after a while, so the sidebar doesn't fill up.

MADE FOR ONE HAND
• The address bar sits at the bottom and scrolls away while you read.
• Swipe it to switch tabs, swipe up to see all of them, tap it to search, open tabs, and run commands.
• On a tablet or a foldable, the sidebar stays docked beside the page.

SYNC THAT ONLY YOU CAN READ
• Spaces, tabs, history, passwords and open tabs sync with Compass on your other devices.
• Everything is encrypted on your device before it leaves it. The sync server stores it encrypted and can't read it.
• No account: pair a device with a code or a QR code shown by Compass on another device, or with your recovery code.
• Send a tab to your other devices.

CLAUDE, WITH YOUR OWN KEY
• Ask Claude about the page you're reading, have it find, click and fill in things, or work across your tabs.
• Claude uses your own Claude API key or endpoint. Your conversations go straight to it, not through us.

THE REST OF A BROWSER
• Extensions from the Chrome Web Store.
• Your saved passwords fill in other apps too.
• Widgets for search, quick actions, Today and your other devices, and a Quick Settings tile for a new tab.
• Incognito tabs, downloads, history, find in page, and the site settings you know from Chrome.

PRIVATE BY DEFAULT
No ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no Google account. Compass turns off Chromium's own data collection, and what you browse stays on your device unless you turn on sync.

This is a test release: things may break. Tell us what you think through Play's feedback.
```
