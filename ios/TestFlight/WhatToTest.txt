Reader mode, sites in spaces, shorter tab titles and answers in the command bar from Claude, Tidy Today and link folders named by Claude, Claude across the whole browser, and spaces that stay on this device.

- Reader mode: on an article (news, blogs, Wikipedia), a book shows at the end of the address bar. Tap it for just the article in clean type; tap it again, or the reader's ×, to go back to the page as it was. Aa changes the font, size, width and colors (Auto, Light, Sepia, Dark) and remembers them; Listen reads it aloud. Also in the page menu (Reader), and ⌘⌥R with a keyboard on iPad. Works in Incognito; nothing is sent anywhere.
- Sites in spaces: Settings → Links from Other Apps → Sites in Spaces → Add Site. A link to that site (or a subdomain) from another app opens as a new tab in the chosen space, and Compass switches to it (on iPad, to a window already showing it); the most specific domain wins. In a little window: ⌄ → Always Open <site> in. Never in Incognito; syncs with your Mac.
- Shorter tab titles: Settings → Claude → Shorter Tab Titles (off by default; needs an API key), with its own model. Long tab titles in the sidebar, like a pull request's or a video's, turn into short ones within a few seconds ("PR #123: fix login"). Only the sidebar's rows change: the page, the command bar and sync keep the full title, and a tab you renamed keeps your name. Never in Incognito.
- Answers in the command bar: Settings → Claude → Answer Questions in the Command Bar (off by default; needs an API key), with its own model. Type a question in the command bar ("how tall is the Eiffel Tower?", "why is the sky blue"): an Ask Claude card shows on top, and Return or a tap asks. The answer appears under the field as Claude writes it. About This Page sends only the shown page's title and site. Continue in Chat opens the Claude chat with the question and answer. Never in Incognito.
- Link folders: long-press a link on a page and choose Open in Background (or ⌘-click it on iPad). The page and its links gather in a Today folder named after the page; more links from it join the folder.
- Names from Claude: Settings → Claude → Name Link Folders (off by default; needs an API key), with its own model. A link folder of 3, 6 and 12 tabs gets a short name from its tabs' titles and sites, with a sparkle. A folder's menu has Undo Name, or Suggest a Name; a folder you rename yourself is never renamed. Never in Incognito.
- Tidy Today: Settings → Claude → Tidy Today (off by default; needs an API key), with its own model. Today's header then shows Tidy next to Clear (with 5 or more loose tabs): Claude groups Today's tabs into a few named folders, from their titles and sites. The message at the bottom says how many, with Undo, which puts every tab back where it was. Folders and Pinned are left alone; never in Incognito.
- Spaces that don't sync: in a space's menu, turn off Sync to Other Devices. The space and its tabs disappear from your other devices and stay only on this one. Turn it back on to sync it again.
- Claude can now use the whole browser as on the Mac (Settings → Claude → Let Claude Use the Whole Browser): ask it to pin, move, rename or group tabs in folders, split two tabs, mute a tab, switch or create a space, search your history, list your downloads, or send a tab to your Mac.
- Please report: articles that get no book (or pages that wrongly do), articles missing text or pictures in Reader, short titles that lose what a tab is about, questions that don't get the Ask Claude card (or searches that wrongly do), and a space that comes back after turning off sync.
