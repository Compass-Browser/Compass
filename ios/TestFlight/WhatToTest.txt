Development mode, for people who build websites.

- Turn it on for the site you're working on: in the page menu, tap Development Mode; or Settings → Development Mode, then add a site (like localhost or example.com); or Cmd+Shift+D on a hardware keyboard.
- On those sites, reload (the button, the address's menu, the page menu and Cmd+R) always gets a fresh copy from the server, never the cache.
- A bar above the page shows its full address, with Copy.
- Turn it off the same way, or remove the site in Settings → Development Mode. The sites stay on this device; they don't sync.
- To inspect a page, use Safari's Web Inspector on a Mac (Develop menu → your iPhone or iPad → the Compass page).
- Please report: pages that still come from the cache after a reload, the bar missing or showing the wrong address, and sites that won't turn on or off.
