Peek at links, and little windows for links from other apps.

- Peek: long-press a link and the preview is the page itself. Tap the preview (or Peek in the menu) to look at it in a card; Open makes it a tab without reloading, closing it leaves nothing behind. Links from pinned tabs and favorites that would open a new tab peek too.
- Little windows: with Compass as your default browser, a link from Mail, Messages or any other app opens in a card over your tabs instead of a new tab. Tap the address to edit it, use back, then Open in your space (or pick another space with the arrow) or close it.
- Settings → Links from Other Apps: turn little windows off to get tabs as before, or turn on "Share Sheet Opens a Little Window" so sharing a link to Compass skips the form and opens it straight away.
- Fixed: extensions' items in the long-press menu were missing.
