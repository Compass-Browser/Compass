Little windows on iPad get a window of their own.

- iPad with windows (Stage Manager, windowed apps or Split View): with Compass as your default browser, a link from another app opens in a small window of its own, centered over your other windows. Open in your space moves the page into your Compass window without reloading; close, Esc or ⌘W closes it. ⌘L edits its address, ⌘O opens it, ⌘[ goes back.
- With Compass full screen, and on iPhone, links from other apps still open in the card, as in the last build.
- Please report: a little window that opens as a full window or doesn't open at all, its back button under the window controls, or an empty window appearing after relaunching Compass.
