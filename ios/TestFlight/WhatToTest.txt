Apple Intelligence for the sidebar, and incognito in the tab switcher.

- Apple Intelligence: Settings → AI in the Sidebar → Answer With can send Name Link Folders, Tidy Today and Shorter Tab Titles to the on-device model. No key needed, and nothing leaves the phone. Ask in the command bar stays with your chat provider.
- Tab switcher: from an incognito tab it shows only incognito tabs, otherwise only regular ones. A Tabs / Incognito toggle beside + switches between them. While incognito is locked, its side shows the lock instead of the tabs.
- Tab switcher +: a tap opens a new tab on the side you're on; holding it offers New Tab, New Incognito Tab and New Easel, as the bar's + does.
- Swiping the bar sideways goes through tabs in the tab switcher's order (most recent first), as the iPhone's app switcher does. The bar is clear Liquid Glass.
- The bar fades out as the tab switcher opens, instead of showing through it.
- The menu's icons (Site Settings, Settings…) keep their colors with a bright space theme in dark mode.
- Please report: incognito tabs showing on the regular side (or the other way round), anything showing while incognito is locked, swipes landing on the wrong tab, and Apple Intelligence answers that look wrong.
