Live folders, split view on the iPhone, and a quicker address bar.

- Live folders: New Live Folder… (beside New Folder, or in the space menu) picks a plugin (GitHub, RSS, Calendar…) and fills the folder with what it finds. It refreshes by itself; a dot marks what's new, and the folder's menu has Refresh, Mark All Seen and Convert to Normal Folder. Live folders sync with Compass on the Mac.
- Split view: long-press a tab and choose Add to Split. A split holds up to four pages, and on the iPhone a split of two shows both at once.
- Long-press the address for Reload, Copy URL, Share, Find on Page and Desktop/Mobile Site.
- 1Password: unlocking with Face ID now presses Unlock instead of showing the password.
