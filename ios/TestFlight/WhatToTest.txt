Claude across the whole browser, iPad keyboard shortcuts and drag and drop, spaces that stay on this device, and picture in picture when you leave the app.

- Spaces that don't sync: in a space's menu, turn off Sync to Other Devices. The space and its tabs disappear from your other devices and stay only on this one. Turn it back on to sync it again.
- Claude's developer tools: Settings → Claude → Let Claude Use Developer Tools (off by default). Then ask Claude about the page: "which requests fail?", "why is this button blue?", "how fast does this page load?", "what's in this site's storage?". Changes to cookies or storage ask you first.
- YouTube: play a video with sound, then switch tab or leave the app; it should keep playing in a picture-in-picture window. Videos now play in the page instead of going fullscreen.
- Leaving the app (swipe home) while a video plays with sound in the page now starts picture in picture on its own; coming back puts the video back in the page. If the window doesn't open or closes at once, please send Settings → Auto Picture in Picture → Copy Diagnostics.
- Claude can now use the whole browser as on the Mac (Settings → Claude → Let Claude Use the Whole Browser): ask it to pin, move, rename or group tabs in folders, split two tabs, mute a tab, switch or create a space, search your history, list your downloads, or send a tab to your Mac.
- Claude through a gateway or custom endpoint no longer fails with "A maximum of 4 blocks with cache_control".
- iPad: with a keyboard, ⌘W should close the tab rather than the window (please tell us if it closes the window), ⌃Tab and ⌃⇧Tab go to the next and previous tab even with the page focused, and extensions' shortcuts work (listed in the Extensions menu). Drag a tab from the sidebar out beside the window to open it in a new window; drag a split pane's title onto another pane to move it, or a sidebar tab onto a pane to add it to the split. Drag the sidebar's right edge to make it wider or narrower (double tap to reset).
- Please report: a space that comes back after turning off sync, pages that load slowly or not at all with developer tools on, or picture in picture that doesn't start (Settings → Auto Picture in Picture → Copy Diagnostics).
