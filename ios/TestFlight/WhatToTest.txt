Sharing spaces, folders and easels with other people, and Compass as your default browser.

- Default browser: in the Settings app, Apps → Default Browser App → Compass. Links from Mail, Messages and other apps then open in Compass, in a little window or in the space a link rule sends them to.
- Share: in the sidebar, touch and hold a pinned folder and tap Share…; for a space, tap ⋯ next to its name; for an easel, ⋯ inside the board. Tap Share This Folder (or space, or easel), pick the role (Editor or Viewer), when the link expires and how many people can use it, then Create Invite Link and send it.
- Join: tap an invite link (with Compass as your default browser it opens straight here; otherwise tap Open in Compass on the page it shows). Pick the space to put it in and tap Join. Shared items have a people badge, and your other devices join too.
- In Sharing…: see who's in, change someone's role or remove them, Leave, or Delete for everyone if you shared it. Set the name others see in Settings → Sharing.
- Viewers can open everything but can't change it.
- Fixed: a new profile on the Mac could stay at "Starting…" forever while syncing.
- Please report: invites that don't open or fail to join, changes that don't show up for the others (and on which devices), and anything that disappears.
