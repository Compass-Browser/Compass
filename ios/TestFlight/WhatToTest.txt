Page translation, on your iPhone.

- On a page in a language you don't read, a translate button shows at the start of the address bar. Tap it to translate; tap again for the original. Long-press it for Translate To, Always Translate and Never Translate.
- Compass translates with its own models (Firefox's, 58 languages), downloaded once per language after asking, with a progress ring in the button; Apple's Translation takes the languages Compass doesn't have. Settings › Translation can put Apple first, and lists the downloaded languages.
- Also: Translate in the page menu, Translate Page in the address bar's menu, ⌘⇧Y with a keyboard.
- Please try a few sites in other languages and tell us about pages that translate badly, lose their layout or links, or never finish.
