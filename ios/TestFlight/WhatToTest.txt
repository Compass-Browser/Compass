More ways to open a link, and Share from the menu button.

- Long-press a link: after Peek, the menu now has Open in New Tab, Open in Background, Open in Incognito, Open in Little Window and Pin. In an incognito tab only Peek, Open in New Tab and Open in Background show, so its links stay incognito.
- Long-press the … button in the bottom bar to share the page you're on.
