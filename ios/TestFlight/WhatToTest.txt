Closed a tab by mistake? You can now bring it back.

- Close a tab (flick its card away, tap × on a Today row, ⌘W, or Clear Today). A bar at the bottom says what closed: tap Undo and it comes back where it was, with its back and forward history.
- Or shake the iPhone: "Undo Close Tab" brings back the last tab you closed, and you can keep shaking to go further back. Redo closes it again.
- With a hardware keyboard, ⌘⇧T reopens the last closed tab.
- While you're typing, a shake still undoes the typing, as usual.
