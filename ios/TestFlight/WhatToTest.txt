Easels: boards of notes, shapes, drawings, images, links and captures of pages, synced with your Mac.

- New Easel: the sidebar's + menu, or "New Easel" in the command bar. The board is pinned in the current space like a tab, with an easel icon; its title follows the board's.
- On a board: add text, rectangles, circles, arrows, freehand drawing, images and links (a link becomes a card with the page's title and picture). Drag empty space to pan, pinch to zoom; Select Area in the ⋯ menu picks several things at once.
- Capture to Easel: in the page menu. Drag over part of the page, then choose the board (or New Easel). The capture keeps the page's address; Refresh in its menu takes it again.
- Boards sync with your other devices, and easels imported from Arc on your Mac show up here.
- Please report: boards that look different here than on the Mac, captures that come out blank or cut, and anything lost while syncing.
