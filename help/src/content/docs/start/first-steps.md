Compass has no tab strip and no toolbar. Everything lives in the sidebar on
the left of the window, and you type addresses and searches in the command
bar. This page is a five-minute tour; each section links to the full guide.

## The sidebar, top to bottom

- **The header:** back, forward and reload, and under them a pill with the
  current site's address. Click the pill to edit the address.
- **Favorites:** a grid of large icons for the sites you use all the time.
  They're the same in every space.
- **The space's name**, such as "Home" or "Work".
- **Pinned:** tabs and folders you want to keep. A pinned tab stays when you
  close it, and opens again where it started.
- **Today:** the tabs you open as you browse, newest first. Tabs you
  haven't looked at for a while (12 hours by default) move to the space's
  archive on their own, so Today stays short. The **Archived tabs** button
  brings them back.
- **New Tab**, at the end of Today.
- **The space switcher**, at the bottom: one button per space, and **+**
  for a new one.

More in [Pinned tabs, Today and Favorites](/guide/sidebar/).

## Open a tab: the command bar

Press <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>T</kbd> (Mac) or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>T</kbd>
(Windows and Linux), or click **New Tab** in the sidebar. The command bar
appears over the page: type an address or a search and press
<kbd>Return</kbd>. It also finds your open and pinned tabs, history,
spaces and commands such as **New space** or **Settings**. Press
<kbd>Esc</kbd> to close it without opening anything.

To change the address of the current tab, press <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>L</kbd> /
<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>L</kbd> or click the address pill.

More in [The command bar](/guide/command-bar/).

## Pin a tab or make it a favorite

- **Pin:** right-click a tab in Today and choose **Pin**, or drag it into
  Pinned.
- **Favorite:** right-click a tab and choose **Add to Favorites**, or drag
  it onto the Favorites grid.
- **Organize:** drag tabs to reorder them, and right-click a folder for
  **New Folder** and **Rename**.

To close a pinned tab's page, click its **−** (**Unload tab**): the entry
stays in the sidebar, dimmed, until you click it again. Closing a favorite
works the same way, from **Close Tab** in its right-click menu.

## Switch spaces

Spaces are separate sets of tabs, each with its own name and colors, such
as one for work and one for home.

- Click a space's button at the bottom of the sidebar.
- On a trackpad, swipe sideways with two fingers on the sidebar.
- Press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>1</kbd> to <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>9</kbd> on a
  Mac, or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>1</kbd> to
  <kbd>9</kbd> on Windows and Linux, for the first nine spaces.

To rename a space, change its colors or icon, double-click its name or
right-click its button. To move a tab to another space, drag it onto the
space's button or right-click it and choose **Move to Space**.

More in [Spaces](/guide/spaces/).

## Split view

To see two tabs side by side, drag one tab onto the middle of another in
the sidebar (to its left or right half), or right-click a tab and choose
**Open in Split View** to put it next to the one you're looking at. A
split can hold up to four tabs. Right-click it and choose **Separate Tabs**
to undo it.

More in [Split view](/guide/split-view/).

## Hide the sidebar

Press <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>S</kbd> (Mac) or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>S</kbd>
(Windows and Linux), or click the sidebar button in its header, to hide the
sidebar and give the page the whole window. While it's hidden, rest the
pointer on the window's left edge to show it for a moment. Press the
shortcut again to bring it back. You can also drag the sidebar's right edge
to make it wider or narrower.

## On iPhone, iPad and Android

The mobile apps have the same spaces, Pinned, Today and Favorites, synced
from your computer when you [turn on sync](/sync/turn-on/):

- **iPhone and Android phones:** the page fills the screen with a small bar
  at the bottom. Swipe up on the address in the bar to open the sidebar,
  left of it for your open tabs, and right of it for the command bar.
  Swipe sideways on the bar to go to the next or previous tab. In the
  sidebar, swipe sideways to change spaces, and long-press an entry to pin
  it, make it a favorite or move it.
- **iPad and Android tablets:** the sidebar is a column on the left, as on
  a computer. With a keyboard, the computer's shortcuts work too, such as
  <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>T</kbd> / <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>T</kbd> and
  <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>S</kbd> / <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>S</kbd>.
