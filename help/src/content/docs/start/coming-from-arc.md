Compass works the way Arc does: a sidebar with spaces, pinned tabs and
favorites, a command bar instead of a toolbar, and links from other apps in
a little window. It can bring over everything you have in Arc, and you
stay signed in to your sites.

Importing from Arc works on Mac and Windows, wherever Arc is installed on
the same computer.

## Import from Arc

The first time you open Compass, it offers to import if it finds Arc on
your computer. To import later:

1. Open the command bar (<kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>T</kbd> /
   <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>T</kbd>) and choose **Import from Another
   Browser**, or open **Settings** and go to **Import from Another
   Browser**.
2. Scroll to the **Arc** section. It lists your Arc profiles, each with
   its spaces and how many tabs they have.
3. If Arc is open, click **Quit Arc**: Compass can't read Arc's data while
   Arc is running.
4. Check the profiles and spaces you want, then click **Import**.
5. On a Mac, macOS asks whether Compass may use **Arc Safe Storage**, the
   key Arc encrypts your logins and passwords with. Choose **Always
   Allow** (or **Allow**), or your cookies and passwords stay behind.
6. When it's done, click **Open my spaces**.

Importing takes a moment for each profile. Your existing Compass spaces
and profiles aren't touched: Arc's are added next to them.

## What comes over

Each Arc profile becomes a Compass [profile](/guide/profiles/) of the same
name, with:

- **Your logins:** cookies, so you stay signed in to your sites.
- **History, passwords, autofill, extensions and settings.**
- **Spaces**, with their names, colors (including gradients) and icons.
- **Pinned tabs and folders**, nested as in Arc, with the names you gave
  your tabs.
- **Today:** Arc's unpinned tabs, with their folders and split views.
- **Favorites**, the icons at the top of Arc's sidebar.
- **The archive:** each space's newest 100 archived tabs.
- **Split views**, as [splits](/guide/split-view/).
- **Muted tabs** stay muted.
- **[Easels](/guide/easels/)** (Mac), with their text, shapes, drawings,
  images, links and captures, where they were in the sidebar.

Tabs come over unloaded: they open when you click them.

:::note[Easels that come over empty]
Arc only keeps an Easel's contents on your Mac after you've opened it
there. If Compass says some Easels came over empty, open them in Arc, quit
Arc, then click **Import Easels Again**.
:::

### What doesn't come over

- Arc's own features without a Compass counterpart: Notes and Library
  media.
- Arc's [Boosts](/guide/boosts/), Air Traffic Control rules and custom
  keyboard shortcuts. Compass has all three, so you can set them up again.
- Tabs archived from Little Arc, Arc's open windows, and your Arc account
  and sync.

On Windows, some cookies and passwords may not be readable. The summary
after the import tells you how many stayed behind.

### Importing again

You can come back to the import page at any time. Profiles you already
imported show as imported, and only the spaces you've added in Arc since
are offered; their logins, history and passwords stay as they were first
imported. Nothing is ever imported twice.

## Arc's features in Compass

| In Arc | In Compass |
|---|---|
| Spaces, Pinned, Today, Favorites, archive | The same: see [Spaces](/guide/spaces/) and [Pinned tabs, Today and Favorites](/guide/sidebar/) |
| Command bar | The [command bar](/guide/command-bar/), with <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>T</kbd> and <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>L</kbd> as in Arc |
| Little Arc | [Little windows](/guide/little-window/): links from other apps, or <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>⌥</kbd> <kbd>N</kbd> (<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>N</kbd> on Windows and Linux) |
| Peek | [Peek](/guide/peek/): Option-click a link on a Mac (Arc used Shift-click) |
| Split View | [Split view](/guide/split-view/), with up to four tabs |
| Air Traffic Control | [Link routing](/guide/link-routing/): **Links from Other Apps** in Settings |
| Boosts | [Boosts](/guide/boosts/), which you can write yourself or ask Claude to make |
| Easels | [Easels](/guide/easels/), on every platform |
| Profiles | [Profiles](/guide/profiles/), one per space, switched in the same window |
| Ask on Page and Arc's AI features | [Claude](/guide/claude/) |
| Arc Sync and Arc for iPhone | [Sync](/sync/turn-on/) and Compass for iPhone, iPad and Android |

### Familiar shortcuts

Compass uses Arc's shortcuts where they differ from Chrome's, for example
<kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>S</kbd> to hide the sidebar, <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>Shift</kbd>
<kbd>C</kbd> to copy the page's link and <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>1</kbd>–<kbd>9</kbd>
to switch spaces. See [Keyboard shortcuts](/guide/shortcuts/) for the
full list, and to change any of them.

## Differences to know

- **Sync without an account.** Compass doesn't have an account to sign in
  to. You pair your devices with a short code, and everything is encrypted
  on your devices. See [Turn on sync](/sync/turn-on/).
- **Peek uses Option-click**, not Shift-click: Shift-click opens a link in
  a new window, as in Chrome.
- **Compass is open source**, and runs on Mac, Windows, Linux, iPhone,
  iPad and Android.
