Compass sync keeps your spaces, pinned tabs, passwords, history and more the
same on all your devices. There's no account to create: your devices form a
**sync chain**, and everything is encrypted on your devices before it leaves
them, so the sync server can't read it.

You start a chain once, on a computer. Then you [add your other
devices](/sync/add-a-device/) to it with a short code.

:::note
You start a chain in Compass on a Mac, Windows or Linux computer, or on
Android. On iPhone and iPad you can only join a chain that another device
started.
:::

## Choose a sync server

Every device in a chain uses the same sync server. You can use:

- **The Compass project's server**, `https://sync.compassbrowser.org/v2`.
  It's invite-only for now: it only serves people who've been added to it.
- **A server you run yourself**, or one run by someone you trust, such as
  your company. See [Run your own sync server](/self-hosting/).

The server address always ends in **`/v2`**, for example
`https://sync.example.com/v2`. If you leave it out, Compass asks you to
"Enter the server's URL, like https://sync.example.com/v2."

Whichever server you use, it only stores data it can't read. See
[Encryption and privacy](/sync/encryption/).

## Start a new chain

<Tabs syncKey="platform">
<TabItem label="Mac">

1. Open the app menu (**⋯**) and choose **Sync is off**, or open
   **Settings** and choose **Sync**.
2. In **Sync server**, type the server's address, for example
   `https://sync.example.com/v2`.
3. Click **Start sync**.
4. Compass shows **Your recovery code**: 12 words. Save them somewhere safe,
   such as a password manager, then click **I saved it**.
5. Click **Restart Compass**. After the restart, sync starts.

</TabItem>
<TabItem label="Windows">

The steps are the same as on a Mac:

1. Open the app menu (**⋯**) and choose **Sync is off**, or open
   **Settings** and choose **Sync**.
2. In **Sync server**, type the server's address.
3. Click **Start sync**.
4. Save the 12 words of **Your recovery code**, then click **I saved it**.
5. Click **Restart Compass**.

</TabItem>
<TabItem label="Linux">

The steps are the same as on a Mac:

1. Open the app menu (**⋯**) and choose **Sync is off**, or open
   **Settings** and choose **Sync**.
2. In **Sync server**, type the server's address.
3. Click **Start sync**.
4. Save the 12 words of **Your recovery code**, then click **I saved it**.
5. Click **Restart Compass**.

</TabItem>
<TabItem label="iPhone and iPad">

You can't start a chain on iPhone or iPad. Start one on a computer, then
[add your iPhone or iPad to it](/sync/add-a-device/).

</TabItem>
<TabItem label="Android">

1. Open the menu, tap **Settings**, then **Sync**.
2. In **Sync server**, type the server's address, for example
   `https://sync.example.com/v2`.
3. Tap **Start a new chain**.
4. Compass shows **Your recovery code**. Keep it somewhere safe: with it,
   any device can join your chain.
5. If Compass asks, tap **Restart**.

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

:::caution[Keep your recovery code]
The recovery code is the only way back to your synced data if you lose
**every** device in the chain. Nobody else has a copy, not even the server.
You can show it again later on any device that syncs. See
[Add a device](/sync/add-a-device/#use-the-recovery-code).
:::

## Check that sync works

On a computer, the app menu shows the sync status above **Settings**:
**Synced** when all is well, or a short message when something needs your
attention. The **Sync** page in Settings shows the same status, with the
time of the last sync.

If it says something else, see [Troubleshooting](/sync/troubleshooting/).
