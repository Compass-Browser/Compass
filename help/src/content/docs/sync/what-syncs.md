Everything below is encrypted on your device before it's uploaded. See
[Encryption and privacy](/sync/encryption/).

## Your sidebar

- **Spaces**: their names, colors and themes, icons, order, and their
  auto-archive setting.
- **Pinned tabs and folders**, including nested folders, custom tab titles
  and split views.
- **Favorites.**
- **Today**: the tabs in each space's Today section. A tab open on one
  device shows on the others as an unloaded tab; click it to load the page
  there. (Arc didn't sync Today; Compass does.)
- **The archive**, for the last 30 days.
- Which folders are open or collapsed.
- Site icons, so the sidebar looks right before a page loads.
- [Easels](/guide/easels/), with their images.
- [Boosts](/guide/boosts/).
- What [Claude](/guide/claude/) remembers about you, if you use its memory.

Some things stay on each device: which tabs are actually loaded, which
window shows what, the width of the sidebar, and whether it's collapsed.

Closing a tab on one device closes it on the others too, except a tab you
are looking at on the other device right now: that one stays open there.
Quitting Compass on one device doesn't close anything on the others.

## Browser data

- **Passwords** (saved passwords and passkeys)
- **History** (pages you visited, and the pages you delete from it)
- **Autofill** (addresses and form entries)
- **Extensions** and their settings
- **Settings** (settings that make sense on every device, and your search
  engines)
- **Open tabs**: your tabs, so other devices can open them. On a computer,
  type in the [command bar](/guide/command-bar/) to find tabs from your
  other devices.
- **Tabs you send** to your other devices. See [Send a tab](/guide/send-tab/).
- **The list of your devices**

Payment cards and other data that needs Google's own services don't sync.

## Turn parts of sync off

Each kind of data has a switch on the **Sync** page, under **What syncs**:
**Spaces**, **Passwords**, **History**, **Autofill**, **Extensions**,
**Settings** and **Open tabs**. Turn off a switch to stop syncing that kind
of data on this device. Data already on the device stays.

:::note
The switches belong to a profile: each profile has its own. See
[Profiles and sync](/sync/profiles/).
:::

The **Spaces** switch covers everything in [Your sidebar](#your-sidebar)
above, including Easels, Boosts and Claude's memory.

## Keep a space on one device

You can keep a single space off sync, for example a space for one computer
only:

- **To make a new space that doesn't sync**, use **New space on this
  device** in the [command bar](/guide/command-bar/). On a Mac, the
  **Spaces** menu also has **New Space on This Device**.
- **To stop syncing an existing space**, open the space's menu and turn off
  **Sync to your other devices**. Compass asks you to confirm, because the
  space is then removed from your other devices (it stays on this one).

A space that doesn't sync shows a small dot on its button in the space
switcher, and its tooltip says **On this device only**. Your favorites and
history still sync, because they belong to the profile, not to the space.

On iPhone and iPad, the space's menu has **Sync to Other Devices**.

## On iPhone and iPad

Compass for iPhone and iPad syncs your spaces and sidebar, passwords (which
you can also use in other apps through AutoFill), history, open tabs and
sent tabs. It shows the extensions you use on your computer and offers to
install them. Autofill entries and settings don't sync to iPhone and iPad.

Android syncs the same data as a computer.
