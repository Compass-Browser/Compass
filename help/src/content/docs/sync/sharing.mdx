You can share a **space**, a **pinned folder** or an **easel** with other
people. Everyone you invite sees it in their own sidebar and, as an
**editor**, changes it with you; a **viewer** only looks. What you share stays
end-to-end encrypted: the sync server stores it but can't read it.

What's shared:

- **A folder**: the folder and everything in it. Each person puts it in the
  space they like.
- **An easel**: the board, its notes, shapes and images. It shows as a pinned
  tab.
- **A space**: its name, color and Pinned. Each person's Today and archive
  stay their own.

Open tabs stay yours: opening a shared tab loads it on your device only.

## Share and invite

<Tabs syncKey="platform">
<TabItem label="Mac">

:::note[In the next update]
1. In the sidebar, right-click a pinned folder and choose **Share…**; for a
   space, open the space's menu and choose **Share space…**; for an easel,
   right-click its tab and choose **Share Easel…**.
2. Click **Start sharing**.
3. Under **Invite with a link**, choose what people can do (**Can edit** or
   **Can view**), when the link expires (7 days, 1 day or never) and how
   many people can use it.
4. Click **Copy invite link** and send it to the people you want.

On a server with company sign-in, you can also **Invite by email**.

Under **Your name for sharing**, set the name others see (it starts as your
computer's user name).
:::

## Join

Open the invite link on a device where Compass syncs. Compass shows what's
shared, from which server, and asks where to put it.

devices join too.

## People, roles and leaving

The share sheet lists everyone, with their role. The person who shared it
(the **owner**) can change a role or remove someone. Removing someone changes
the key: they keep the copy they have, but don't see changes from then on.

- **Leave** stops sharing it with you. Your copy stays, as an ordinary
  folder, space or easel.
- **Delete for Everyone** (the owner) ends the sharing for everyone. Everyone
  keeps a copy.

If you're removed, a note above the sidebar tells you, and your copy stays.

<Tabs syncKey="platform">
<TabItem label="Mac">

:::note[In the next update]
Right-click the shared folder and choose **Sharing…** (for a space: the
space's menu, **Sharing…**; for an easel: its tab, **Share Easel…**). Under
**People**, the owner changes a role or clicks **Remove**. At the bottom:
**Leave**, or **Delete for everyone** for the owner.

A viewer's shared items can't be renamed, moved or edited; the people badge's
tooltip says **view only**.
