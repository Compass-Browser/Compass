Compass updates itself. You don't need to download new versions by hand. To install Compass in the first place, see [Install Compass](/start/install/).

## How updates arrive

<Tabs syncKey="platform">
  <TabItem label="Mac">
    Compass checks for updates every few hours and downloads them in the background, without interrupting you.

    Once an update is ready, the app menu shows **Relaunch to update**. Choose it to restart into the new version: your spaces and tabs come back. If you'd rather not restart now, just keep going: the update is installed the next time you quit Compass.

    To check right away, open **Settings** → **About Compass**.

    :::note[Installed with Homebrew?]
    `brew upgrade` leaves Compass alone, and that's expected: Compass updates itself, as described above.
    :::
  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="Windows">
    Compass checks for updates a couple of minutes after it starts and then every few hours, and installs them in the background.

    Once an update is installed, **Settings** → **About Compass** offers **Relaunch** right away, and after a while the app menu shows **Relaunch to update**. Restarting switches to the new version, and your spaces and tabs come back.

    To check right away, open **Settings** → **About Compass**.
  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="Linux">
    If you installed the Flatpak, Compass updates with your other Flatpak apps: run `flatpak update`, or use your software center's updates. The new version is used the next time you start Compass.

    The tarball doesn't update itself. To update it, download the new version and replace your copy.
  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="iPhone and iPad">
    Compass for iPhone and iPad comes through TestFlight. TestFlight tells you when a new build is available, and you can install it from the TestFlight app (or turn on automatic updates there).
  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="Android">
    Compass for Android updates through Google Play, like your other apps.
  </TabItem>
</Tabs>

## Update channels

On Mac and Windows, you can choose how early you get new versions:

| Channel | What you get |
|---|---|
| **Stable** | Releases once they've been on Beta. The default |
| **Beta** | New features before Stable, plus Stable releases when they're newer |
| **Canary** | New builds first, plus Beta and Stable releases when they're newer. Expect rough edges |

Every release starts on Canary and moves up to Beta and then Stable once it has held up, without being rebuilt. So Stable is always a build that Canary and Beta users have already run.

To change your channel:

1. Open **Settings** → **About Compass**.
2. Under **Update channel**, choose **Stable**, **Beta** or **Canary**.

Compass checks for updates right away. Moving to a more stable channel never installs an older version: Compass stays on the version you have until that channel catches up.

## Version numbers

Compass versions are the date and time the release was made, as `YYYYMMDD.HHMM`. For example, `20261120.0052` was made on November 20, 2026, at 00:52. A larger number is always newer.

**Settings** → **About Compass** shows Compass's version along with the version of Chromium it's built on.
