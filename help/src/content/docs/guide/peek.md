Peek shows a link's page in a card over the page you're on. Close it and
nothing is left behind; keep it and it becomes a tab, without reloading.

## Peek at a link

- **Mac:** hold <kbd>⌥ Option</kbd> and click the link.
- **Windows and Linux:** hold <kbd>Alt</kbd> and click the link.

The page opens in a rounded card and the page behind it dims. The card's
title and site show above it; hover over them to see the full address.

Links in [pinned tabs and favorites](/guide/sidebar/) that would open a new
tab also open in Peek, so your pinned apps stay put. <kbd>⌘</kbd>-click /
<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>-click and middle click still open a tab in the background.

## Close it

Any of these closes the card:

- <kbd>Esc</kbd> (unless the page uses it first),
- <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>W</kbd> / <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>W</kbd>,
- the **×** next to the card,
- a click on the dimmed area, the sidebar or anywhere else in the window,
- switching tabs or spaces.

Switching to another app leaves it open.

## Keep it as a tab

Click the open button next to the card (**Open as Tab**), or press
<kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>O</kbd> / <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>O</kbd>. The page becomes a
new tab in Today, with its history, scroll position and anything you typed.

## Inside the card

- Links you click load in the card.
- Links that open a new tab or window open as tabs in the window, and the
  card closes.
- The window's keyboard shortcuts still work, such as <kbd>⌘</kbd>
  <kbd>T</kbd> / <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>T</kbd>.

The card has no back button, and pop-up dialogs from the page in the card
don't show.

## On iPhone and iPad

Long-press a link. The preview is the page itself: tap it, or choose
**Peek** at the top of the menu, to open it in Peek's card without
reloading. The same menu also has **Open in New Tab**, **Open in
Background**, **Open in Incognito**, **Open in Little Window** and **Pin**.

- Close the card with its close button or by swiping it down (on an iPad,
  also by tapping outside it). With a keyboard, <kbd>Esc</kbd> or
  <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>W</kbd>.
- Tap **Open** (or press <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>O</kbd>) to keep the page as the
  newest tab in Today.

Links from pinned tabs and favorites that would open a new tab peek too.

## On Android

Long-press a link and choose **Peek**, at the top of the menu. The card
rises from the bottom (on a tablet, it's centered over the window).

- Close it with **×**, the back gesture, a tap on the dimmed area, or by
  dragging its bar down.
- Tap **Open** (or press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>O</kbd>) to keep the page as
  a new tab in Today.

Links from pinned tabs and favorites that would open a new tab peek too.
