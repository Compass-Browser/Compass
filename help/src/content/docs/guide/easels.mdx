An **Easel** is a free-form board: put text, shapes, freehand drawings, images, link cards and captures of web pages anywhere on it. Easels live in your sidebar like tabs and sync to all your devices.

If you're coming from Arc, your Arc Easels come over with the Arc import on the Mac. See [Coming from Arc](/start/coming-from-arc/).

## Make an Easel

<Tabs syncKey="platform">
<TabItem label="Mac">
- Click the **New Easel** button in the **Pinned** header of the sidebar, or
- Type **New Easel** in the [command bar](/guide/command-bar/).
</TabItem>
<TabItem label="Windows">
- Click the **New Easel** button in the **Pinned** header of the sidebar, or
- Type **New Easel** in the [command bar](/guide/command-bar/).
</TabItem>
<TabItem label="Linux">
- Click the **New Easel** button in the **Pinned** header of the sidebar, or
- Type **New Easel** in the [command bar](/guide/command-bar/).
</TabItem>
<TabItem label="iPhone and iPad">
Touch and hold **+** (in the sidebar or the bottom bar), then tap **New Easel**.
</TabItem>
<TabItem label="Android">
In the sidebar, open the space's **⋯** menu and tap **New Easel**.
</TabItem>
</Tabs>

The new board, called **Untitled Easel**, is pinned in the current space. Click its title at the top to rename it; the sidebar follows.

An Easel's tab moves, splits, archives and syncs like any other tab. Closing or archiving the tab doesn't delete the board.

## All your Easels

**All Easels** (the **Easels** link at the top of a board) lists every board, with **New Easel**, **Rename** and **Delete**. Deleting a board here removes it from all your devices.

## Add things to a board

On the desktop and Android, pick a tool from the toolbar, or press its key:

| Tool | Key |
|---|---|
| **Select** | <kbd>V</kbd> |
| **Text** | <kbd>T</kbd> |
| **Rectangle** | <kbd>R</kbd> |
| **Circle** | <kbd>O</kbd> |
| **Arrow** | <kbd>A</kbd> |
| **Pen** (freehand) | <kbd>P</kbd> |
| **Image** | <kbd>I</kbd> |

You can also:

- **Paste**: an image becomes an image, a link becomes a link card (with the page's title and picture), other text becomes text.
- **Drop** files, images or links onto the board.
- Double-click an empty spot to add text there.

Text and shapes come in a small palette of colors. Text has styles (title, heading, body, caption) and fonts, including a handwriting one.

Changes save as you make them.

## Arrange and move around

- **Select**: click an item, drag over several, or <kbd>Shift</kbd>-click to add to the selection. <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>A</kbd> / <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>A</kbd> selects everything.
- Right-click (or long-press) an item for **Open**, **Refresh**, **Edit Text**, **Bring to Front**, **Send to Back**, **Duplicate** and **Delete**.
- Keys: <kbd>]</kbd> brings to front, <kbd>[</kbd> sends to back, <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>D</kbd> / <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>D</kbd> duplicates, arrow keys nudge.
- **Pan**: drag an empty spot while holding <kbd>Space</kbd> (or with the middle mouse button), or scroll. On a touch screen, drag with one finger.
- **Zoom**: <kbd>⌘</kbd> / <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> and the mouse wheel, or pinch. <kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>1</kbd> fits everything on screen, <kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>0</kbd> goes to 100%.
- Click a link card or a capture to open its page in a new tab.

Each device remembers where you were looking on each board.

### On iPhone and iPad

The editor is built for touch: drag an empty spot to pan, pinch to zoom, tap to select, drag to move, and use the corner handle to resize. Double-tap text to edit it. The selection bar has color, text style and font, front, back, duplicate and delete. The **⋯** menu has **Select Area**, **Paste**, **Fit**, **Undo**, **Redo**, **Background** and **All Easels**. Tap the photo button to add a picture.

## Capture to Easel

Capture part of any web page and put it on a board. The capture remembers its page, so you can open it or refresh it later.

<Tabs syncKey="platform">
<TabItem label="Mac">
1. Right-click the page and choose **Capture to Easel…**, or type **Capture to Easel** in the command bar.
2. Drag over the part of the page you want (<kbd>Esc</kbd> cancels).
3. Under **Add to Easel**, pick a board, or **New Easel**.
</TabItem>
<TabItem label="Windows">
1. Right-click the page and choose **Capture to Easel…**, or type **Capture to Easel** in the command bar.
2. Drag over the part of the page you want (<kbd>Esc</kbd> cancels).
3. Under **Add to Easel**, pick a board, or **New Easel**.
</TabItem>
<TabItem label="Linux">
1. Right-click the page and choose **Capture to Easel…**, or type **Capture to Easel** in the command bar.
2. Drag over the part of the page you want (<kbd>Esc</kbd> cancels).
3. Under **Add to Easel**, pick a board, or **New Easel**.
</TabItem>
<TabItem label="iPhone and iPad">
1. Open the page menu and tap **Capture to Easel**.
2. Drag over the part of the page you want, then tap **Capture**.
3. Pick a board, or **New Easel**.
</TabItem>
<TabItem label="Android">
1. Open the page menu and tap **Capture to Easel**.
2. Pick a board, or **New Easel**.

On Android, the capture is the whole visible page.
</TabItem>
</Tabs>

The board you used last is at the top of the list. A short message tells you where the capture went.

Capture to Easel isn't available in Incognito.

### Refresh a capture

Captures are pictures of the page at the time you took them. To update one, right-click it (or touch and hold it) and choose **Refresh**. Compass loads the page in the background, with your sign-ins, and captures the same area again.

Refreshing captures isn't available on Android yet.

## Sync

Easels sync with your other devices as part of your spaces. If two devices edit different things on the same board, both changes are kept. See [What syncs](/sync/what-syncs/).
