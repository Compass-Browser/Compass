A space is a sidebar of its own: its own Pinned tabs and folders, its own
Today and its own archive. You might keep one space for work, one for home
and one for a side project. A window shows one space at a time, and the
tabs of the other spaces stay open in the background, right where you left
them.

Your first space is called **Home**. [Favorites](/guide/sidebar/#favorites)
aren't part of a space: they stay the same as you switch, as long as the
spaces belong to the same [profile](/guide/profiles/).

## Create a space

<Tabs syncKey="platform">
<TabItem label="Mac">
- Click **+** at the end of the space switcher, at the bottom of the
  sidebar, or
- open the command bar (<kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>T</kbd>) and choose **New space**,
  or
- choose **Spaces** > **New Space** in the menu bar.

The new space is called "Space 2", "Space 3" and so on, and the window
switches to it. [Rename it](#rename-a-space-and-choose-an-icon) whenever you
like.
</TabItem>
<TabItem label="Windows">
- Click **+** at the end of the space switcher, at the bottom of the
  sidebar, or
- open the command bar (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>T</kbd>) and choose **New
  space**.

The new space is called "Space 2", "Space 3" and so on, and the window
switches to it.
</TabItem>
<TabItem label="Linux">
- Click **+** at the end of the space switcher, at the bottom of the
  sidebar, or
- open the command bar (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>T</kbd>) and choose **New
  space**.

The new space is called "Space 2", "Space 3" and so on, and the window
switches to it.
</TabItem>
<TabItem label="iPhone and iPad">
Open the sidebar and tap **+** at the end of the space icons at the bottom.
</TabItem>
<TabItem label="Android">
Open the sidebar and tap **+** at the end of the space icons in its bottom
bar.
</TabItem>
</Tabs>

:::tip
**New space on this device** (in the command bar, and **New Space on This
Device** in the Mac's Spaces menu) makes a space that doesn't sync to your
other devices. See [What syncs](/sync/what-syncs/).
:::

## Switch spaces

<Tabs syncKey="platform">
<TabItem label="Mac">
- Click a space's button in the switcher at the bottom of the sidebar.
- Swipe left or right with two fingers on the sidebar to move to the next
  or previous space.
- Press <kbd>Control</kbd> <kbd>1</kbd> to <kbd>Control</kbd> <kbd>9</kbd>
  for the first nine spaces.
- Choose a space from the **Spaces** menu in the menu bar.
- Type the space's name in the command bar.
</TabItem>
<TabItem label="Windows">
- Click a space's button in the switcher at the bottom of the sidebar.
- Swipe left or right with two fingers on the sidebar (on a touchpad).
- Press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>1</kbd> to
  <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>9</kbd> for the first nine spaces.
- Type the space's name in the command bar.
</TabItem>
<TabItem label="Linux">
- Click a space's button in the switcher at the bottom of the sidebar.
- Swipe left or right with two fingers on the sidebar (on a touchpad).
- Press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>1</kbd> to
  <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>9</kbd> for the first nine spaces.
- Type the space's name in the command bar.
</TabItem>
<TabItem label="iPhone and iPad">
- In the sidebar, swipe sideways to page between spaces, or tap a space's
  icon at the bottom.
- With a hardware keyboard, press <kbd>⌃</kbd> <kbd>1</kbd> to
  <kbd>⌃</kbd> <kbd>9</kbd>.
</TabItem>
<TabItem label="Android">
In the sidebar, swipe sideways to go to the next space, or tap a space's
icon in its bottom bar.
</TabItem>
</Tabs>

You can change the space shortcuts in **Settings** > **Keyboard shortcuts**
(see [Keyboard shortcuts](/guide/shortcuts/)).

To change the order of your spaces on a computer, drag a space's button
along the switcher. The shortcuts and swipes follow the new order.

## The space menu

Everything about a space lives in its menu.

- **Mac, Windows and Linux:** right-click the space's button in the
  switcher, or double-click the space's name at the top of the sidebar.
- **iPhone, iPad and Android:** tap the **⋯** button next to the space's
  name in the sidebar.

### Rename a space and choose an icon

On a computer, the space's name is the field at the top of the menu: type a
new name and press <kbd>Return</kbd> / <kbd>Enter</kbd>. On iPhone, iPad and
Android, choose **Rename**.

Below **Edit theme**, a computer's space menu lists icons for the space's
button: no icon (the name's first letter), a set of symbols, and an
**Emoji** field. Type or pick any emoji there; on a Mac,
<kbd>Fn</kbd> <kbd>E</kbd> opens the emoji picker. The icon shows on your
phone too.

### Colors and themes

Each space can have its own color, which tints the sidebar and themes the
whole window.

1. Open the space menu and choose **Edit theme** (**Edit Theme** on iPhone,
   iPad and Android).
2. In the theme editor:
   - Pick automatic, light or dark mode for the browser's own interface.
     Web pages keep your system's light or dark setting.
   - Drag the big dot around the color wheel to change the hue and
     saturation. Use **−** and **+** to use one, two or three colors for a
     gradient; the small dots are the gradient's other colors.
   - Or pick a ready-made color or gradient from the palette, or any color
     with the color picker.
   - Adjust the intensity of the tint and the amount of grain.
3. Changes show right away. On a computer, press <kbd>Esc</kbd> or click
   the space's name at the top of the editor to go back to the menu.

Grey, the default, leaves the window untinted. Incognito windows and
profiles that use an extension theme keep their own colors.

### Archive delay

Tabs in Today that you haven't looked at for a while move to the space's
archive on their own. Choose how long in the space menu: **Archive Today
tabs after** on a computer (**Archive Tabs** on iPhone and iPad, **Archive
Tabs After…** on Android): 12 hours (the default), 24 hours, 7 days, 30
days, or never. More in [The archive](/guide/sidebar/#the-archive).

### Profile

A space can belong to any of your profiles, so that its tabs use that
profile's cookies, history and favorites. On a computer, choose the profile
in the space menu's **Profile** list. See [Profiles](/guide/profiles/).

### Delete a space

On a computer, click **Delete space** in the space menu, then click again
(**Click again to delete**) to confirm. On iPhone, iPad and Android, choose
**Delete Space**.

The space's Pinned tabs, Today and archive go with it, and its open tabs
close. You can't delete your only space.

:::caution
Deleting a space closes its open tabs without asking, even pages with
unsaved changes.
:::

## Move tabs to another space

- **Mac, Windows and Linux:** drag a tab, folder or split onto another
  space's button in the switcher, or right-click it and choose **Move to
  Space**. An open tab moves as it is, without reloading.
- **iPhone and iPad:** long-press the entry and choose **Move to Space**.
- **Android:** long-press the entry and choose **Move to** followed by the
  space's name.

A pinned entry goes to the end of the other space's Pinned, a Today entry
to its Today. Tabs can only move between spaces of the same profile.

## Links from other apps

You can send links to certain sites, such as GitHub or YouTube, straight to
a space of your choice when you open them from another app. See
[Link routing](/guide/link-routing/).
