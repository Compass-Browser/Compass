A **live folder** is a pinned folder that fills itself: the latest posts of a feed, the pull requests waiting for your review, your next meetings with their call links. It refreshes on its own and marks what's new.

Live folders work on Mac, Windows, Linux, iPhone and iPad. They aren't available on Android yet.

## What can fill a live folder

Each live folder gets its entries from a **plugin**. Compass comes with these:

| Plugin | Shows |
|---|---|
| **RSS Feed** | The latest posts from an RSS, Atom or JSON feed. |
| **GitHub** | Pull requests and issues that need you: review requests, your pull requests, assignments, mentions or any search. Needs a GitHub token. |
| **Calendar** | Upcoming events with their meeting links (Meet, Zoom, Teams…), from a calendar's private iCal address (Google, Outlook, iCloud). |
| **Links from a Page** | Links on a web page that match some text or a pattern. It can use your login on that site. |

## Make a live folder

<Tabs syncKey="platform">
<TabItem label="Mac">
1. In the sidebar, click the **New live folder** button in the **Pinned** header (next to **New folder**). Or right-click a pinned folder and choose **New Live Folder…**.
2. Pick a plugin.
3. Fill in the form: a **Name** (optional) and the plugin's settings, such as the feed's address or how many items to show.
4. Click **Create**.
</TabItem>
<TabItem label="Windows">
1. In the sidebar, click the **New live folder** button in the **Pinned** header (next to **New folder**). Or right-click a pinned folder and choose **New Live Folder…**.
2. Pick a plugin.
3. Fill in the form: a **Name** (optional) and the plugin's settings, such as the feed's address or how many items to show.
4. Click **Create**.
</TabItem>
<TabItem label="Linux">
1. In the sidebar, click the **New live folder** button in the **Pinned** header (next to **New folder**). Or right-click a pinned folder and choose **New Live Folder…**.
2. Pick a plugin.
3. Fill in the form: a **Name** (optional) and the plugin's settings, such as the feed's address or how many items to show.
4. Click **Create**.
</TabItem>
<TabItem label="iPhone and iPad">
1. In the sidebar, tap **New Live Folder…** (next to New Folder), or choose it from the space's menu.
2. Pick a plugin, fill in its settings and save.
</TabItem>
</Tabs>

Live folders live in **Pinned**. You can't drag your own tabs into one, but you can drag an entry out: it becomes an ordinary pinned tab.

### Tokens, private addresses and logins

- Some plugins ask for a secret, such as a GitHub token or your calendar's private address. Secrets are **kept on this device only**: they never sync. On each of your other devices, you enter them once.
- A plugin that can use your login on a site (such as **Links from a Page**) asks first: check **Send your cookies for…** to let it see what you see when signed in. You decide this for each folder.

## Using a live folder

- The folder refreshes on its own every few minutes (each plugin sets how often), when you expand it and when you switch to its space.
- New entries have a dot. A collapsed folder shows how many are new.
- If a refresh fails, the folder shows an error mark; point at it to see why.

Right-click the folder (touch and hold on iPhone and iPad) for:

- **Refresh**: check for new entries now.
- **Edit Source…**: change the plugin's settings.
- **Mark All Seen**: clear the new-entry dots.
- **Convert to Normal Folder**: stop updating and keep the current entries as ordinary pinned tabs.

## Sync

The folder itself (its plugin and settings) syncs to your other devices. The entries don't: each device runs the plugin and fills the folder on its own. If a plugin you installed isn't on another device yet, **Edit Source…** offers to install it there.

## Add more plugins

Anyone can write a plugin in JavaScript. To install one:

<Tabs syncKey="platform">
<TabItem label="Mac">
- **From the web**: in the live folder plugin picker, click **Install a plugin…** and enter the address of the plugin's `manifest.json`. Click **Review** to see which sites it reaches, which ones it may send your cookies to and what secrets it asks for, then **Install**.
- **From a folder on your computer**: click **Open plugins folder** in the picker and put the plugin's folder (with its `manifest.json`) in it. It shows in the picker as **Local**.
</TabItem>
<TabItem label="Windows">
- **From the web**: in the live folder plugin picker, click **Install a plugin…** and enter the address of the plugin's `manifest.json`. Click **Review** to see which sites it reaches, which ones it may send your cookies to and what secrets it asks for, then **Install**.
- **From a folder on your computer**: click **Open plugins folder** in the picker and put the plugin's folder (with its `manifest.json`) in it. It shows in the picker as **Local**.
</TabItem>
<TabItem label="Linux">
- **From the web**: in the live folder plugin picker, click **Install a plugin…** and enter the address of the plugin's `manifest.json`. Click **Review** to see which sites it reaches, which ones it may send your cookies to and what secrets it asks for, then **Install**.
- **From a folder on your computer**: click **Open plugins folder** in the picker and put the plugin's folder (with its `manifest.json`) in it. It shows in the picker as **Local**.
</TabItem>
<TabItem label="iPhone and iPad">
- **From the web**: in **New Live Folder**, tap **Install a Plugin…**, enter the address of the plugin's `manifest.json`, tap **Review** to see what it reaches, then **Install**.
- **By hand**: copy the plugin's folder into **Files** › **On My iPhone** (or iPad) › **Compass** › **Live Folder Plugins**. New Live Folder lists it as installed by hand.
</TabItem>
</Tabs>

To remove an installed plugin, click the **×** next to it in the picker. Folders that used it stay, and say the plugin is missing. Only install plugins from people you trust: a plugin can reach the sites it lists, and your logins there if you allow it.

If you want to write your own plugin, see the [plugin guide on GitHub](https://github.com/Compass-Browser/Compass/blob/main/docs/live-folder-plugins.md).
