When Compass is your default browser, links you open in other apps go to a
[little window](/guide/little-window/). With link routing, links to the
sites you choose open in a space of yours instead: GitHub links from Slack
in your Work space, YouTube links from Mail in Home.

## How rules work

- A rule names a **site** and a **space**, for example `github.com` →
  Work.
- A rule covers the site's subdomains: `github.com` also takes
  `gist.github.com`. A rule for a subdomain wins over its site's rule, so
  you can send `gist.github.com` to Notes and the rest of GitHub to Work.
- A matching link opens as a new tab in Today of the rule's space, and
  Compass comes to the front. If no window shows that space, your
  frontmost window switches to it (or a new window opens).
- Only links **from other apps** are routed. Links you click inside
  Compass stay where they open.
- Links never open in incognito through a rule.
- Rules sync with your spaces. If a rule's space is deleted, its links go
  back to opening in a little window, and the rule shows as **Space
  deleted** until you edit or delete it.

Type a site's domain, such as `github.com`. You can also paste a whole
address, and Compass keeps just the site.

## Add a rule

<TabItem label="Mac">
**From a little window:** click the **⌄** button next to **Open in**, choose
**Always Open** followed by the site's name, then the space. The page moves

**From a tab:** open the command bar (<kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>T</kbd> or

## Change or delete a rule

- **Mac, Windows and Linux:** in **Settings** > **Links from Other Apps**,
  click **Edit** or **Delete** next to the rule.
- **iPhone and iPad:** in **Sites in Spaces**, tap a rule to edit it, or
  swipe it to delete it.
- **Android:** in **Sites in Spaces**, tap a rule to change its space or
  delete it.

## Things to know

- On a computer, the rules and spaces in Settings are those of the profile
  of the window you opened Settings from. A rule can only send links to a
  space of its own profile.
- On iPhone and iPad, links shared to **Compass Browser** from the share
  sheet are routed too when **Share Sheet Opens a Little Window** is on.
- Links from other apps open in a new tab of the shown space instead of a
  little window when **Open in a Little Window** is off (iPhone, iPad and
  Android). Rules still apply.
