Found something on your computer you want to read on the go? Send the tab
to your phone. It shows up there as a notification; tap it and the page
opens in Compass.

Sending needs [sync](/sync/turn-on/) on both devices, in the same sync
chain. Your devices appear in the list once they've synced; devices you
haven't used for more than 10 days aren't listed.

## Send a tab from your computer

1. In the sidebar, right-click the tab.
2. Choose **Send to Your Devices**, then the device.

A short confirmation appears once the tab is on its way. You can also send
the page from the menu you get when you right-click the page itself.

## Receive it on iPhone or iPad

The tab arrives as a notification, **Tab from** followed by the name of the
computer it came from, with the page's title and address. Tap it to open
the page in Compass.

- Compass asks for permission to show notifications the first time it
  opens with sync on. If you said no, turn notifications on for Compass in
  the iOS Settings app.
- If Compass is open, the tab arrives right away. Otherwise it usually
  arrives within seconds through a push notification; without push, it
  arrives the next time iOS lets Compass refresh in the background, or when
  you open Compass.
- Tabs sent more than 10 days ago aren't shown.

:::note
Push notifications depend on your sync server. If you run your own, see
[Push notifications](/self-hosting/push/) for how to set them up.
:::

## Android and other computers

- **Android:** sent tabs arrive as notifications too. To send a link from
  Android, long-press it and use the menu's send-to-devices item.
- **Computers:** a tab sent to another computer shows up in Compass there
  the way it does in Chrome.

The page's address and title are encrypted end to end, like everything
else Compass syncs: the server can't read where you're sending people.
