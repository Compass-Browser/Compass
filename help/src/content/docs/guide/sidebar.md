The sidebar replaces the tab strip. From top to bottom, it shows the
address pill, your **Favorites**, the space's name, **Pinned**, **Today**,
**New Tab** and the space switcher. Everything in it belongs to the current
[space](/guide/spaces/), except Favorites, which every space of a
[profile](/guide/profiles/) shares.

## Today

New tabs land at the top of **Today**. Think of it as your desk for the day:
open what you need, and let Compass tidy up after you.

- Close a tab with the **×** on its row, middle-click it, or press
  <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>W</kbd> / <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>W</kbd>. It leaves
  Today.
- **Clear** in Today's header (it shows while the pointer is over the
  header) closes every Today tab at once. Pinned tabs stay.
- Tabs you haven't looked at for a while move to the
  [archive](#the-archive) on their own.
- Links you open in the background from a page (<kbd>⌘</kbd>-click /
  <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>-click or middle click) gather with that page in a Today
  folder named after it, so a page's tabs stay together.

After you quit and reopen Compass, Today's tabs are still there. They load
when you click them.

## Pinned tabs

**Pinned** is for the tabs you keep: your mail, your calendar, documents
you come back to.

To pin a tab, do one of these:

- Drag it from Today into Pinned.
- Right-click it and choose **Pin**.
- Open the command bar and choose **Pin tab**.

To unpin, drag it back into Today, or right-click it and choose **Unpin**.

### Open and unloaded pinned tabs

A pinned tab stays in the sidebar even when its page is closed. A closed
pinned tab is shown dimmed (unloaded); click it to load it again.

- On an open pinned tab, the button on its row is **−** (**Unload tab**). It
  closes the page and keeps the entry. <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>W</kbd> /
  <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>W</kbd> does the same.
- On an unloaded pinned tab, the button is **×**, which removes it from the
  sidebar. Click it twice: the first click turns it red (**Click again to
  remove**), the second removes it. A double-click works too.
- Right-click a pinned tab and choose **Remove from Pinned** to remove it
  at once.

### The pinned URL

A pinned tab remembers the page you pinned. Browse away from it, then
close it, and it goes back to that page. While a pinned tab shows another
page, its right-click menu offers:

- **Use This Page as Pinned URL**, to make the current page the one it
  keeps;
- **Back to Pinned URL**, to go back to the page it was pinned with.

Favorites have the same items, as **Use This Page as Favorite URL** and
**Back to Favorite URL**.

## Folders

Folders keep Pinned and Today organized, and they can nest.

- **New folder:** click **+** in the Pinned or Today header, or right-click
  a folder and choose **New Folder**.
- **Rename:** double-click the folder, press <kbd>F2</kbd> on it, or
  right-click it and choose **Rename**.
- **Collapse or expand:** click the folder.
- **Delete:** right-click it and choose **Delete Folder**. Its open tabs
  close.

A Today folder closes by itself once its last tab leaves (closed, archived,
pinned or moved). Pinned folders stay, even empty.

## Favorites

Favorites are the big icons at the top of the sidebar, for the sites you
use all the time. They're the same in every space of a profile.

- **Add:** right-click a tab and choose **Add to Favorites**, drag it onto
  the Favorites grid, or use **Add to Favorites** in the command bar.
- **Close its page:** right-click the icon and choose **Close Tab**. The
  favorite stays.
- **Remove:** right-click the icon and choose **Remove from Favorites**.

## Rearrange the sidebar

Drag tabs, folders and splits to reorder them, into folders, between
Pinned and Today, or onto another space's button in the switcher. Drop a
link from a page or another app into the sidebar to open it there.

Drag an entry out of every Compass window and drop it on the desktop to
open it in a [little window](/guide/little-window/).

## Right-click menu

Right-click any entry for more. Depending on the entry, you'll find:

- **Rename**, to give a tab your own name (<kbd>F2</kbd> or a double-click
  works too). Clear the name to go back to the page's title.
- **Copy Link** (without tracking parameters, unless you've turned that
  off in Settings), **Duplicate** and **Reload**.
- **Mute Tab** / **Unmute Tab**. A tab playing sound also shows a speaker;
  click it to mute the tab.
- **Pin** / **Unpin**, **Add to Favorites** and **Move to Space**.
- **Open in Split View** and **Separate Tabs**: see
  [Split view](/guide/split-view/).
- **Send to Your Devices**: see [Send a tab to your phone](/guide/send-tab/).
- **Close Tab**, **Remove from Pinned**, **Remove from Favorites**, or
  **Remove** for an unloaded Today tab.

## The archive

Each space keeps an archive of the Today tabs that went unseen for a while
(12 hours by default). Tabs that are on screen or playing sound are never
archived, and pinned tabs never are.

- Click the **Archived tabs** button in Today's header to see them, newest
  first, with how long ago they were archived.
- Click one to bring it back to Today.
- Click its **×** to drop it, or **Clear** to empty the archive.

To change the delay, or turn archiving off, see
[Archive delay](/guide/spaces/#archive-delay). The archive keeps the last
100 tabs.

## Undo closing

Closed something by mistake? Compass can bring back tabs, pinned tabs,
folders, and even a whole **Clear**, with their history.

- **Mac:** press <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>Z</kbd> (when you're not typing in a
  text field) to undo, and <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>⇧</kbd> <kbd>Z</kbd> to close
  it again. <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>⇧</kbd> <kbd>T</kbd> (**Reopen Closed Tab**)
  also undoes.
- **Windows and Linux:** press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>T</kbd>.

## Hide the sidebar

Press <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>S</kbd> / <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>S</kbd> to collapse
the sidebar to a narrow strip and give the page more room; press it again
to bring it back. On a Mac, it's also in the **View** menu. You can also
drag the sidebar's edge to make it wider or narrower.

## Media playing in other tabs

When a tab that's out of sight plays music or video, a small player appears
at the bottom of the sidebar, above the space switcher, with play/pause,
previous, next and mute. Click the tab's icon to go to it, or **×** to
pause and hide the player.

## Keyboard

Press <kbd>Tab</kbd> to move into the sidebar's list, then use the arrow
keys to move between entries. <kbd>→</kbd> and <kbd>←</kbd> expand and
collapse folders, <kbd>Return</kbd> or <kbd>Space</kbd> opens a tab, and
<kbd>Delete</kbd> closes it.

## Incognito windows

Incognito windows have Today and Favorites, but no Pinned: nothing in
incognito is kept after you close its windows.

## On iPhone, iPad and Android

The phone and tablet apps show the same spaces, Pinned, Today, Favorites
and archive, synced from your computer.

- **iPhone:** the sidebar is a panel over the page. Tap the sidebar button
  in the bottom bar, or swipe up on the address. Swipe it down to half
  height to see the page, or all the way down to close it.
- **iPad:** in a wide window, the sidebar is a column on the left. The
  sidebar button on the bar hides or shows it, and so does <kbd>⌘</kbd>
  <kbd>S</kbd> with a keyboard. Drag its edge to resize it.
- **Android:** swipe up on the bar's address to open the sidebar. On a
  tablet it stays on screen as a pane.

Long-press an entry for its menu: **Pin**, **Unpin**, **Archive**, **Add to
Favorites**, **Rename**, **New Folder**, the pinned URL items and more.
Long-press and drag to reorder. Closing a Today tab on a phone or tablet
sends it to the space's archive. The archive opens from the space's **⋯**
menu (**Archive**).

On iPhone and iPad, a toast with **Undo** appears after you close
something, and you can also shake the phone to undo. On Android, the
snackbar's **Undo** brings it back.
