A profile keeps its own cookies and sign-ins, history, passwords,
favorites and extensions. In Compass, profiles belong to
[spaces](/guide/spaces/): each space is assigned to a profile, and all
your spaces, whatever their profile, share one space switcher.

So a "Work" space can use your work profile and a "Personal" space your
personal one. Switch between them like any two spaces, in the same window:
Compass changes the profile in place, without opening another window.

## Assign a space to a profile

On a Mac, Windows or Linux computer:

1. Open the space menu: right-click the space's button in the switcher, or
   double-click the space's name.
2. In the **Profile** list, choose a profile, or **New profile…** to create
   one.

When you have more than one profile, each space button's tooltip names its
profile.

What happens when a space changes profile:

- Its pinned tabs, Today, folders, colors, icon and archive come along.
- Open tabs can't carry their sign-ins to another profile, so they close.
  The page you were looking at reloads in the new profile; the other tabs
  come back unloaded and load when you click them.
- You may need to sign in to sites again, since the new profile has its
  own cookies.

## What changes when you switch to another profile's space

- **Favorites** are the profile's, so the Favorites grid changes to that
  profile's.
- **Extensions** are the profile's, so the extension buttons in the sidebar
  header change too.
- **History**, saved passwords and site settings are the profile's.
- The command bar finds every space, but only the open and pinned tabs of
  the window's current profile.

## Things to know

- Tabs can't move between spaces of different profiles. **Move to Space**
  lists only spaces of the same profile, and dragging a tab onto another
  profile's space does nothing.
- When you start Compass and your spaces use more than one profile, the
  window opens on the first space in the switcher, unless Compass restores
  your previous session.
- Incognito windows only show their own spaces.
- On a computer, [link routing](/guide/link-routing/) rules are kept per
  profile.

## On iPhone, iPad and Android

Your profiles and their spaces come from your computer through sync; see
[Profiles and sync](/sync/profiles/). The sidebar lists every profile's
spaces, and each space's tabs use its profile's website data.

To move a space to another profile, open the space's **⋯** menu and choose
**Move to Profile** (iPhone and iPad) or **Move to** followed by the
profile's name (Android). Compass warns you first that its tabs will be
signed out of sites, because each profile keeps its own cookies.
