**Reader** shows just the article: the text and its pictures, in clean type, without ads, menus or pop-ups. It covers the page in the same tab, so leaving Reader brings the page back exactly as it was.

Everything happens on your device: the page is never sent anywhere. Reader works in Incognito too.

## Show Reader

When a page looks like an article, a **book** button appears at the end of the address.

<Tabs syncKey="platform">
<TabItem label="Mac">
- Click the book button at the right of the address at the top of the sidebar (**Show Reader**), or
- Press <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>⌥</kbd> <kbd>R</kbd>, or
- Type **Reader Mode** in the [command bar](/guide/command-bar/).
</TabItem>
<TabItem label="Windows">
- Click the book button at the right of the address at the top of the sidebar (**Show Reader**), or
- Press <kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>R</kbd>, or
- Type **Reader Mode** in the [command bar](/guide/command-bar/).
</TabItem>
<TabItem label="Linux">
- Click the book button at the right of the address at the top of the sidebar (**Show Reader**), or
- Press <kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>R</kbd>, or
- Type **Reader Mode** in the [command bar](/guide/command-bar/).
</TabItem>
<TabItem label="iPhone and iPad">
- Tap the book at the end of the address in the bottom bar, or
- Touch and hold the address and tap **Show Reader**, or
- Tap **Reader** in the page menu, or
- With a keyboard, press <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>⌥</kbd> <kbd>R</kbd>.
</TabItem>
<TabItem label="Android">
- Tap the book at the end of the address in the bottom bar, or
- Touch and hold the address and tap **Show Reader**, or
- Tap **Reader** in the page menu, or
- With a keyboard, press <kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>R</kbd>.
</TabItem>
</Tabs>

The shortcut and the menu work on any page. If Compass can't find an article, Reader says so.

## Leave Reader

Any of these takes you back to the page:

- The book button again (**Hide Reader**), or the same shortcut.
- The **×** at the top of the article (**Leave Reader**), or **Original page** at the end.
- <kbd>Esc</kbd>.
- Clicking a link in the article. It loads in the tab and leaves Reader. Links you open in a new tab (<kbd>⌘</kbd>-click / <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>-click, or a middle click) leave Reader up.
- Going back, reloading or loading another page in the tab.

Reader is per tab: switch to another tab and back, and the article is still there. In a split, each side can be in Reader or not.

## Change how it looks

Click **Aa** (**Text and colors**) at the top of the article:

- **Font**: **Serif** or **Sans**.
- **Text size**: the small and large **A** buttons.
- **Width**: **Narrow**, **Medium** or **Wide**.
- **Theme**: **Auto** (follows your system's light or dark mode), **Light**, **Sepia** or **Dark**.

Compass remembers your choices for the next article.

## Listen to an article

Click **Listen** at the top of the article to have it read aloud, with your system's own voices. The button only shows when your device has a voice for the article's language. The text stays on your device.

## Good to know

- On the desktop, **Find** (<kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>F</kbd> / <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>F</kbd>) and **Print** use the page underneath, not the article. On iPhone and iPad, **Find on Page** searches the article.
- [Little windows](/guide/little-window/) don't have Reader.
- You can change Reader's shortcut in [Keyboard shortcuts](/guide/shortcuts/) (it's listed as **Reader mode**).
