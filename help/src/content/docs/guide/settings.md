On Mac, Windows and Linux, Compass's settings are part of the browser's **Settings** page, next to the usual browser settings (passwords, privacy, search engine, appearance and so on). On iPhone, iPad and Android, Compass has its own settings screen.

## Open Settings on a computer

- **Mac:** choose **Compass** → **Settings…** in the menu bar, or press <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>,</kbd>.
- **Windows and Linux:** open the app menu and choose **Settings**.
- **Anywhere:** open the [command bar](/guide/command-bar/), type "settings" and choose **Settings**.

The command bar can also take you straight to some sections: **Keyboard shortcuts**, **Import from Another Browser**, **Route this site to space…** (opens Links from Other Apps with a rule for the current site) and **Boost this site…** (opens Boosts for the current site).

## Compass's sections

Compass adds these sections to the menu on the left of **Settings**, right after **You and Google**:

| Section | What's there |
|---|---|
| **Sync** | Turn on sync, add devices, choose what syncs. See [Turn on sync](/sync/turn-on/) |
| **Keyboard shortcuts** | See and change Compass's shortcuts. See [Keyboard shortcuts](/guide/shortcuts/) |
| **Links from Other Apps** | Which space links from other apps open in, per site. See [Link routing](/guide/link-routing/) |
| **Boosts** | Your per-site styles and scripts. See [Boosts](/guide/boosts/) |
| **Import from Another Browser** | Bring in tabs, bookmarks and more from Arc, Chrome and other browsers. See [Import from another browser](/start/browser-import/) |
| **Claude** | Your AI provider and key, and what Claude may do. See [Claude in Compass](/guide/claude/) |

The search box at the top of **Settings** searches Compass's sections too.

:::note[No "On startup" section]
Chrome's **On startup** section isn't in Compass. You don't need it: Compass's sidebar always brings back your spaces and their tabs when it opens.
:::

The **About Compass** page (at the bottom of the menu) shows your version and, on Mac and Windows, lets you pick an update channel. See [Updates](/guide/updates/).

## iPhone and iPad

Tap the button at the bottom left of the sidebar (its icon shows your sync status), or open the page menu and tap **Settings**. Highlights:

- **Search Engine**, **Passwords**, **AI** (see [Claude in Compass](/guide/claude/)), **Boosts** and **Extensions**.
- **On Launch**: start on the **Sidebar** or on the **Last Tab** you had open.
- **Archive Inactive Tabs**, **Clear Browsing Data** and **Remove Tracking from Copied Links**.
- **Auto Picture in Picture**, see [Picture in picture](/guide/picture-in-picture/).
- **Links from Other Apps** and **Sites in Spaces**, see [Link routing](/guide/link-routing/) and [Little window](/guide/little-window/).
- **Sync**, see [Add a device](/sync/add-a-device/).
- **Global Site Settings**, **Privacy Policy** and **Third-Party Licenses**.

## Android

Tap the gear in the sidebar, or open the page menu and tap **Settings**. The screen is grouped into:

- **Sync** (and **Profiles**, when you sync more than one).
- **Browsing**: **Default Browser**, **Open in a Little Window**, **Sites in Spaces**, **Search Engine**, **Passwords**, **Passwords in Other Apps**, **Claude** and **Extensions**.
- **Tabs and data**: **On Launch**, **Clear Browsing Data** and **Downloads**.
- **Privacy**: **Site Settings**, **Privacy and Security** and **Privacy Policy**.
- **General**: **Accessibility**, **Languages**, **More Settings** (the rest of the browser's settings) and **Third-Party Licenses**.

Your Compass version is shown at the bottom.
