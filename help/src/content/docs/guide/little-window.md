When Compass is your default browser, a link you click in another app
(Mail, Slack, a chat, a terminal) doesn't land among your tabs. It opens in
a **little window**: read it and close it, or keep it by moving it into a
space.

A little window has back, the page's address (click it to type another
one) and an **Open in** button naming a space. The page isn't in your
sidebar until you move it there, and little windows aren't restored after
you quit.

## Keep the page

- Click the **Open in** button (it names a space, such as **Open in
  Home**), or press <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>O</kbd> /
  <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>O</kbd>. The page moves, as it is and without
  reloading, into your last used window as a new tab in Today, and the
  little window closes.
- To pick another space, click the **⌄** button next to it (**Open in
  Another Space**) and choose a space. The menu lists the spaces of the
  same profile. It shows when you have more than one space, or when the
  page is on a website.

If you always want a site's links in a particular space, choose **Always
Open** followed by the site's name, such as **Always Open github.com in**,
in the same **⌄** menu, then the space. From then on,
links to that site from other apps skip the little window and open in that
space. See [Link routing](/guide/link-routing/).

To close the page instead, close the little window as you would any
window.

## Open an empty little window

Press <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>⌥</kbd> <kbd>N</kbd> (Mac) or
<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>N</kbd> (Windows and Linux). An empty
little window opens with its address field ready for typing.

This shortcut works from any app, even when Compass isn't in front, as
long as Compass is running. You can change it in **Settings** > **Keyboard
shortcuts** ("New little window").

:::note
On Linux, the shortcut works from other apps only under X11. Under Wayland
it works in Compass's own windows. If another app already uses the same
keys, the shortcut only works inside Compass.
:::

## Other ways to get one

Drag a tab from the sidebar and drop it outside every Compass window: it
opens there in a little window.

## Where it appears

- **Mac:** the little window opens over the app the link came from, on the
  same display, without bringing Compass's other windows to the front. It
  has no title bar: the window controls sit in its top bar.
- **Windows and Linux:** it opens on the display where your pointer is,
  with the system's usual title bar above Compass's bar.

## On iPhone and iPad

With Compass as your default browser, a link from another app opens in a
card over whatever Compass is showing. Its bar has back, the address (tap
it to type another one), **Open in** with the shown space's name, and a
**⌄** menu with your other spaces and **Always Open** for the site. Close the card to let the page
go; iOS's back button in the status bar takes you back to the app the link
came from.

A second link replaces the first. With a keyboard, <kbd>⌘</kbd>
<kbd>O</kbd> keeps the page and <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>W</kbd> closes it.

On an iPad, when Compass is one of several windows on screen (Stage
Manager or Split View), the little window opens as a window of its own.

Settings > **Links from Other Apps** has two switches:

- **Open in a Little Window** (on by default). Turn it off to open links
  from other apps as new tabs in Today instead.
- **Share Sheet Opens a Little Window** (off by default). Turn it on to have
  **Compass Browser** in the share sheet open the link in a little window
  straight away.

## On Android

With Compass as your default browser, a link from another app opens in a
card over the window, with ×, back, the address, **Open in** with the space's name and a
**⌄** menu, as on iPhone. <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>O</kbd> keeps the page.

Turn it off in Settings > **Browsing** > **Open in a Little Window**. Apps
that open links in their own in-app browser keep doing so.
