A split shows two, three or four tabs next to each other in the same
window. In the sidebar, a split is one entry: its tabs share a row, and you
can pin it, move it to another space or put it in a folder like any tab.

## Make a split

On a computer, do one of these:

- **Drag a tab onto another one.** Drop it on the middle of the other tab's
  row: on its left half to put the dragged tab on the left, on its right
  half to put it on the right. The half you're over is highlighted. (Dropping
  on the top or bottom edge of a row moves the tab instead.)
- **Use the menu.** While you look at one tab, right-click another tab in
  the sidebar and choose **Open in Split View**. The two open side by side.
- **From a link.** Right-click a link on a page and choose the option to
  open it in split view.

To add a third or fourth tab, drag it onto one of the split's tabs in the
sidebar, or right-click it and choose **Open in Split View** while the
split is on screen.

## Work in a split

- Click in a tab to make it the active one: the address pill, back, forward
  and the keyboard shortcuts act on it.
- Drag the divider between two tabs to give one more room.
- Each tab's menu at the top of its pane can arrange the split side by side
  or stacked.
- Click a tab in the split's sidebar row to show the split with that tab
  active.

## End a split

- **Separate the tabs:** right-click the split (or one of its tabs) and
  choose **Separate Tabs**. They become ordinary tabs again, in the split's
  place.
- **Close it:** right-click the split and choose **Close Split**.
- Closing one tab of a split of three or four keeps the others together.

A pinned split stays a split when you close it: click it later and all its
tabs open together.

## On iPhone, iPad and Android

Splits you make on a computer sync to your phone and tablet, and you can
make them there too.

- **Make one:** long-press a tab in the sidebar and choose **Split With**
  (**Split With…** on Android), then the other tab. **Add to Split**
  (**Add to Split…** on Android) adds a tab to a split next to it, up to
  four. On Android, **Separate Tabs** ends a split.
- **On a phone,** a split of two shows both tabs, one above the other (side
  by side in landscape), each under a title strip. The front tab is
  outlined and the bar acts on it; tap the other tab's strip to bring it to
  the front. A split of three or four shows one tab at a time, with a
  picker at the top to switch.
- **On an iPad,** a split shows its tabs side by side when there's room.
  Drag a tab's title strip onto another's to rearrange them, or drop a tab
  from the sidebar onto a strip to add it to the split.
- **On Android,** drag the handle between the two tabs to resize them.
