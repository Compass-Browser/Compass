When you leave a tab whose video is playing with sound, Compass shows the video in a small **picture-in-picture** window that floats over everything. Come back to the tab and the video goes back into the page. You don't have to press anything.

## When it happens

The video moves into the floating window when you:

- switch to another tab,
- switch to another space or profile,
- leave the Compass window: cover it with another app or window, or minimize it (desktop).

If several tabs are playing, the one you left last is shown.

It doesn't happen for:

- muted videos or muted tabs,
- pages that aren't secure (plain `http`),
- pages with more than one video playing, or very small videos.

A picture-in-picture window you opened yourself (from a site's own button) stays until you close it.

## Calls

Calls in **Microsoft Teams**, **Google Meet** and the **Zoom** web app follow you too:

- **Teams and Zoom**: the window shows the person speaking, and switches as someone else talks. It has buttons for your **microphone**, **camera** and **hang up**.
- **Google Meet**: Meet's own mini window, with its participants and controls.

## The window

- **Back to tab** (top left) takes you to the video's tab, even in another space.
- Close and minimize buttons are at the top right.
- Playback controls are in the middle, and the timeline, mute and captions at the bottom.
- On the desktop, the window floats over every app, including apps in full screen on a Mac.

### Move and resize it (Mac and Windows)

- **Drag** it anywhere. Let go near an edge of the screen and it snaps neatly to that edge or corner.
- With a trackpad (a precision touchpad on Windows): **pinch** to resize it and **swipe with two fingers** to move it.

### Click through it (Mac and Windows)

Need to click what's underneath without moving the window? Press <kbd>⌃</kbd> <kbd>⌥</kbd> <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>P</kbd> on a Mac or <kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>P</kbd> on Windows, from any app. The window lets clicks through and fades when the pointer is over it. Press the same keys again to make it clickable.

Click-through stays on for the next picture-in-picture windows until you turn it off. You can change the keys in [Keyboard shortcuts](/guide/shortcuts/) (**Let clicks through picture-in-picture**). Click-through isn't available on Linux.

## Turn it off

On the desktop:

- **Only when leaving the window**: go to **Settings** › **Privacy and security** › **Site settings** › **Automatic picture-in-picture** and turn off **Also when you leave the window**. Switching tabs still opens the window.
- **For one site**: block automatic picture-in-picture for that site in the same place, or in the site's settings.

## On iPhone and iPad

- Leaving a tab with a playing video, or leaving the app (going to the Home Screen), puts the video in picture-in-picture. Coming back puts it back in the page.
- **Scrolling** a playing video off the screen does too, and scrolling back puts it in the page.
- Videos play in the page instead of jumping to full screen; the player's own full-screen button still works.
- YouTube videos keep playing in the window.
- Teams and Zoom calls show the largest participant's video. Meet's mobile site sends you to its app.
- The window's button to return to the page brings the video back, still playing; its close button pauses it.

To turn it off, go to **Settings** › **Auto Picture in Picture**. **When Scrolled Away** turns off just the scrolling part.

## On Android

Switching away from a tab with a playing video opens picture-in-picture, and switching back closes it. Leaving the Compass app doesn't start it yet.

To turn it off, go to **Settings** › **Site Settings** › **Automatic Picture in Picture**.
