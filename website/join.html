Sharing in Compass

In Compass Browser you can share a space, a folder or an easel with other people. Everyone sees the same tabs and boards, kept in sync and end-to-end encrypted: the sync server can't read them. Share from the item's menu, and whoever opens the invitation link in Compass can join.
