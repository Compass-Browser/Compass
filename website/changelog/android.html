Compass for Android is in testing on Google Play: canary builds go to internal testing first, then to the testing program as beta. Every build is also an APK on GitHub.

20261121.1035 Canary

21 November 2026 · Chromium 157.0.8103.51

Sharing with other people, and company sign-in for sync.

  • Share spaces, folders and easels with other people: touch and hold a pinned folder and tap Share…, tap … next to a space’s name, or touch and hold an easel’s tab. Invite people as editors or viewers with a link that can expire or be limited to a number of people. Everyone sees the same tabs and boards, end-to-end encrypted: the sync server can’t read them.
  • Join from an invite link: open it, choose Compass if Android asks, pick the space to put it in and tap Join. Set the name others see under “Your name for sharing”.
  • Company sign-in for sync servers: a self-hosted sync server can ask for a sign-in with your company’s identity provider (OIDC); Compass shows the sign-in and a code to check.
  • Fixed: a new profile could stay at “Starting…” forever while syncing.

20261118.1710

18 November 2026 · Chromium 157.0.8103.51

The first build on Google Play.

  • Peek: long-press a link to look at it without opening a tab.
  • Links from other apps open in a little window over what you’re doing.
  • Reader: tap the book in the address bar to read an article on its own.
  • A playing video goes picture-in-picture when you switch tabs.
  • The bottom bar floats over the page and hides as you scroll.
  • Link folders gather the links you open from a page.
  • Claude names folders, tidies Today and shortens tab titles.
  • Compass can be your default browser.

20261113.0903 Canary

13 November 2026 · Chromium 157.0.8103.51

  • Privacy and Site Settings are Compass’s own pages: Safe Browsing, third-party cookies, secure connections, preloading and Do Not Track, and each permission’s default for every site.
  • History and Downloads open Compass’s own screens: search your history by day and remove visits; follow, pause, resume, open and delete downloads.
  • Tabs on other devices show up as you type in the command bar.
  • Pin to Compass from the share sheet lets you choose where the link goes.
  • Widgets: Quick Actions, Today and Other Devices, besides search.
  • Save passwords from other apps with Compass as your autofill app.

The first Compass for Android.
