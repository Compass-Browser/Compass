What's new on iPhone and iPad

Compass for iPhone and iPad is in beta on TestFlight. These are the builds sent to beta testers.

20261111.2141 Beta

11 November 2026

Claude remembers you, and Spotlight finds your spaces.

  • Claude memory. Claude can save short facts about you (preferences, what you work on) and uses them in later chats. Each save shows in the chat with Undo. Settings → Claude → Memory lists them to edit, delete or add, with switches to turn memory or saving off. Memories sync with Compass on the Mac.
  • Spotlight. Search from the Home Screen for a space's name to open it, or for any favorite or pinned tab (all of them now, not just the first few). Searching "web" or "internet" finds Compass.

20261110.2150 Beta

10 November 2026

Live folders, split view on the iPhone, and a quicker address bar.

  • Live folders. New Live Folder… (beside New Folder, or in the space menu) picks a plugin (GitHub, RSS, Calendar…) and fills the folder with what it finds. It refreshes by itself; a dot marks what's new, and the folder's menu has Refresh, Mark All Seen and Convert to Normal Folder. Live folders sync with Compass on the Mac.
  • Split view. Long-press a tab and choose Add to Split. A split holds up to four pages, and on the iPhone a split of two shows both at once.
  • Long-press the address for Reload, Copy URL, Share, Find on Page and Desktop/Mobile Site.
  • 1Password: unlocking with Face ID now presses Unlock instead of showing the password.

20261107.1623 Beta

7 November 2026

Closed a tab by mistake? You can now bring it back.

  • Close a tab (flick its card away, tap × on a Today row, ⌘W, or Clear Today). A bar at the bottom says what closed: tap Undo and it comes back where it was, with its back and forward history.
  • Or shake the iPhone: "Undo Close Tab" brings back the last tab you closed, and you can keep shaking to go further back. Redo closes it again.
  • With a hardware keyboard, ⌘⇧T reopens the last closed tab.

20261107.1556 Beta

7 November 2026

Pinned tabs and favorites now keep a pinned URL, as on the Mac.

  • Pin a tab (or add a favorite), then browse elsewhere in it. Close it (flick its card away or ⌘W): it goes back to the page it was pinned with.
  • While a pinned tab or favorite is on another page, long-press it in the sidebar: "Use This Page as Pinned URL" makes the current page its new home, "Back to Pinned URL" returns to it.
  • The pinned URL syncs with Compass on the Mac.
