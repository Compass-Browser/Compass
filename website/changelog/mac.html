20261121.1535 Canary

21 November 2026 · Chromium 157.0.8103.51

  • Tab previews in the sidebar: hover a tab and its card shows what’s on the page instead of a screenshot. A Google Calendar tab shows your next events (calendars in English for now), a GitHub tab your latest notifications. Plugins can add previews for other sites; they read the page apart from it and can’t send what they read anywhere.
  • Notification counts on tabs: a GitHub tab shows how many notifications you haven’t read, as a count at the end of its row (a dot on a favorite), kept current in the background. To see the count and the notifications from any GitHub page, turn on “Advanced: use your session on github.com” for GitHub in Settings › Plugins; without it, the tab shows a dot when something is unread.
  • Settings › Plugins: every live folder and tab preview plugin in one place, with the sites each one reads, its own settings, its source, and Install and Remove.
  • Claude can make plugins: with “Let Claude make plugins” on in Settings › Claude, right-click a tab and choose Make a Preview for…, or describe a live folder to Claude. It looks at the page, tests the plugin on your tab, and asks before saving it; you can undo it in the chat.
  • Development mode, for web developers: press Cmd+Shift+D on a site you’re working on (or use its switch in the site information popup, the command bar, or Settings › Development Mode). On that site, reload always skips the cache, a bar above the page shows the full address (which you can edit) with buttons for the Console, the Network panel, picking an element and copying the address, and the Network panel lists the requests made before DevTools opened.
  • Fixed: saving a key for OpenAI or another provider no longer erases your Anthropic key, endpoint and model.
  • Fixed: a call could stay out of picture-in-picture when you left its tab right after a video tile turned.
  • Fixed: menus in the Share sheet closed as soon as they opened.

20261121.1035 Canary

21 November 2026 · Chromium 157.0.8103.51

  • Share spaces, folders and easels with other people: right-click a pinned folder and choose Share…, choose Share space… in a space’s menu, or Share Easel… on an easel’s tab. Invite people as editors or viewers with a link (it can expire or be limited to a number of people), or by email on a server with company sign-in. Everyone sees the same tabs and boards, end-to-end encrypted: the sync server can’t read them. Opening an invite link in Compass shows what it is and lets you pick the space to put it in.
  • Company sign-in for sync servers: a self-hosted sync server can ask for a sign-in with your company’s identity provider (OIDC). Compass shows “Sign in to … to sync” and a code to check, and remembers the sign-in for the chain. Servers without it work as before.
  • Install a .crx from its developer: with Developer mode on in chrome://extensions, a .crx opened from a link installs like one dropped on that page (uBlock Origin’s GitHub builds, for example). With Developer mode off, the message now says to turn it on.
  • Fixed: a new profile could stay at “Starting…” forever when syncing with a server that lists its allowed chains.

20261120.2050 Beta

20 November 2026 · Chromium 157.0.8103.51

  • Easels: boards of notes, shapes, drawings, images, link cards and captures of pages, in the sidebar like a tab. Make one with the Pinned header’s easel button or “New Easel” in the command bar. “Capture to Easel” (the command bar or a page’s right-click menu) lets you drag over part of a page and add it to a board; Refresh on a capture takes it again. Easels sync with your other devices, and Import from Arc brings your Arc easels along.
  • Manifest V2 extensions keep working, among them the full uBlock Origin: they install from their developers (the Chrome Web Store no longer lists them), and ones Chromium had turned off come back.
  • Remove a device from sync: Settings → Sync, Remove next to a device. It stops syncing and leaves your list; what’s on it stays there, and it can join again with a pairing code.
  • Fixed: a device that left sync and joined again came back with its old identity.

20261117.2106 Canary

17 November 2026 · Chromium 157.0.8103.51

  • Share a Boost: Share on a Boost in Settings → Boosts copies a link or saves a .compassboost file. Opening a Boost link, or Import… with a file, shows its name, site and all its code before you add it; it’s added as a new Boost marked Shared and never replaces one of yours. 20261115.2355 Canary

15 November 2026 · Chromium 157.0.8103.51

  • Boosts: your own CSS and JavaScript for a site, applied every time you visit it. Make one in Settings → Boosts or with “Boost this site” in the command bar, or ask Claude to make one (turn on “Let Claude make Boosts” in Claude’s settings first). Claude saves style changes straight away with an Undo; any JavaScript waits for your approval, showing you the code. Boosts sync with your other devices.

20261115.0534 Canary

15 November 2026 · Chromium 157.0.8103.51

  • Reader mode. On a page that reads as an article, a Reader button in the address area shows just the article in clean type, in the same tab, with your choice of font, size and theme. Esc or the button again goes back. Also in the command bar ("Reader Mode").
  • Import from Another Browser replaces Import from Arc (Settings, the command bar and first run): Chrome, Edge, Brave, Vivaldi, Dia, Firefox and Zen, and Safari. Each profile becomes a new space: bookmarks become pinned tabs, open tabs land in Today, and history comes along; passwords too from Chrome, Edge, Brave and Vivaldi.
  • Links from other apps go to a space (Settings → Links from Other Apps): pick a space for a site, and links to it opened from other apps open there instead of in a little window. Make a rule from the little window's space menu ("Always Open … in") or with "Route this site to space…" in the command bar. Rules sync.
