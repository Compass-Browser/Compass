Beta releases come first; the stable channel gets them once they've settled. Every release is on GitHub.

20261110.0956 Beta

10 November 2026 · Chromium 156.0.8078.48

  • Live folders. A folder in the sidebar that fills itself from a plugin (GitHub, RSS, Calendar and more), refreshes on its own and marks what's new. Live folders sync with your other devices.
  • Live folder plugins of your own: put them in the Compass Plugins folder (Open Plugins Folder takes you there).
  • Calls in picture in picture. Leave a Teams, Google Meet or Zoom call's tab or window and the call follows you in a small window, with a button to hang up.

20261109.0957 Beta

9 November 2026 · Chromium 156.0.8078.48

  • Settings say "About Compass" in every language, and Help opens Compass's issues on GitHub.

20261109.0200 Beta

9 November 2026 · Chromium 156.0.8078.48

  • Split view with up to four tabs. Separating a split of three or four puts every tab back in the sidebar.
  • In the sidebar and in little windows: middle-click closes a tab, tabs drag out into their own window, and spaces show their icons.
  • The address in a little window can be edited.
  • Picture in picture has Compass's own controls, with a "Back to tab" title and a steady timer.
  • A new window has its space's colors from the start.

20261108.2255 Beta

8 November 2026 · Chromium 156.0.8078.48

  • Claude accepts the token from claude setup-token, to use your Claude subscription (after you turn on "Accept the risks of subscription tokens").
  • With the sidebar shown on hover, its collapse button sits after the window buttons.
  • The grain on space colors is fainter on non-Retina displays.
  • Compass cleans up leftover profile copies when it starts.

20261108.1023 Beta
