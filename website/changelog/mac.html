Canary builds come first, for testing, then beta; the stable channel gets them once they've settled. A build that's on both canary and beta is marked Beta. Every release is on GitHub.

20261114.1035 Canary

14 November 2026 · Chromium 157.0.8103.51

  • Claude through a gateway that adds its own cache settings now works too: Compass tries with fewer, then none, and remembers what worked for that endpoint.

20261114.0011 Canary

14 November 2026 · Chromium 157.0.8103.51

  • Spaces that don't sync: in a space's menu, turn off "Sync to your other devices" to keep it, and its tabs, on this Mac only, or make one with New Space on This Device in the command bar or the Spaces menu. A dot on the space's button in the switcher marks it.
  • Claude through a gateway or custom endpoint no longer fails with "A maximum of 4 blocks with cache_control".
  • Larger icons on the Pinned and Today headers.

20261113.2258 Canary

13 November 2026 · Chromium 157.0.8103.51

  • Claude's developer tools (Settings → Claude, off by default): Claude can look at a page's network requests, elements and styles, storage and performance, and emulate devices and slow networks. Changes to cookies or storage ask you first.
  • Undocked developer tools open as a window only, not as a tab in the space.
  • Picture in picture: the controls at the bottom stay inside the window on videos with no next track, such as YouTube's.

20261113.1930 Canary

13 November 2026 · Chromium 157.0.8103.51

  • Claude and files. In Claude's settings, Files lets Claude save files (into Downloads or a folder you choose) or work in one folder, listing, reading and writing files there. Each save or change asks you first: Allow, Allow for This Chat, or Deny. In Claude Code mode with a folder, Claude Code can also use its own tools there, asking the same way.
  • A Spaces menu in the menu bar, between Tab and Window: switch spaces, with their shortcuts, or make a new one.
  • The menu bar leaves out what Compass doesn't have: the toolbar toggles in View, Home in History, Group Tab and the Profiles menu.
  • Picture in picture: click-through stays on, for the next videos and after a restart, until you turn it off with its shortcut.

20261112.2006 Canary

12 November 2026 · Chromium 157.0.8103.51

  • A model menu in the Claude chat's header: pick another model and the conversation goes on with it.
  • A simpler app menu: no profile row, tab groups, Search Tabs or Your Devices, which spaces, folders and the command bar take care of.
  • Settings no longer have an On Startup section: the sidebar brings your spaces and tabs back by itself.
  • Compass's logo in place of Chrome's in Settings, Safety Hub and the certificate manager, and no more gap where its ribbons cross.

20261111.1908 Canary

  • Claude memory. Claude can save short facts about you (preferences, what you work on) and uses them in later chats. Each save shows in the chat with Undo, and Settings lists them to edit, delete or add, with switches to turn memory or saving off. Memories sync with your other devices.

20261110.0956 Beta

10 November 2026 · Chromium 156.0.8078.48

  • Live folders. A folder in the sidebar that fills itself from a plugin (GitHub, RSS, Calendar and more), refreshes on its own and marks what's new. Live folders sync with your other devices.
  • Live folder plugins of your own: put them in the Compass Plugins folder (Open Plugins Folder takes you there).
  • Calls in picture in picture. Leave a Teams, Google Meet or Zoom call's tab or window and the call follows you in a small window, with a button to hang up.
