20261111.1908 Canary

11 November 2026 · Chromium 157.0.8103.51

  • Chromium 157.
  • Claude memory. Claude can save short facts about you (preferences, what you work on) and uses them in later chats. Each save shows in the chat with Undo, and Settings lists them to edit, delete or add, with switches to turn memory or saving off. Memories sync with your other devices.
  • New Live Folder has its own icon, a folder with a bolt, beside New Folder.

20261110.0956 Beta

10 November 2026 · Chromium 156.0.8078.48

  • Live folders. A folder in the sidebar that fills itself from a plugin (GitHub, RSS, Calendar and more), refreshes on its own and marks what's new. Live folders sync with your other devices.
  • Live folder plugins of your own: put them in the Compass Plugins folder (Open Plugins Folder takes you there).
  • Calls in picture in picture. Leave a Teams, Google Meet or Zoom call's tab or window and the call follows you in a small window, with a button to hang up.
