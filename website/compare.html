Compass compared with other browsers

How Compass compares with Arc, its successor Dia, and the browsers people move to from Arc. Written on 10 November 2026; the Compass column updated and the other columns checked on 14 November 2026. Other browsers change quickly, so check their sites before relying on a cell.

  • The Compass column comes from this repository's code (main).
  • The Crest column comes from its site, changelog and repository.
  • The other columns come from public reviews and the vendors' sites.

✅ yes · ~ partial or different · ❌ no · ? not confirmed by any source found · — not applicable

Arc's status

The Browser Company announced on 22 October 2026 that Arc stops working on 22 December 2026: no updates, and all accounts deactivated. New Arc accounts are already blocked, Arc Search loses its AI features, sign-in and tab sync on 23 November, account data requests close on 15 December, and Arc Search for Android leaves Google Play on 22 December. Personal data is deleted within 30 days of the shutdown. The team's successor is Dia, on Mac and, since October 2026, Windows (The Browser Company is part of Atlassian since October 2025).

What only Compass has

As far as the sources show:

  • Sync without an account, end-to-end encrypted, on a server anyone can run.
  • Claude built in, with the user's own key, a compatible gateway, or a Claude subscription through Claude Code; the same tools as an MCP server.
  • Live folders as JavaScript plugins meant to run on desktop, Android and iPhone.
  • Picture-in-picture for Teams, Meet and Zoom calls, with their controls.

Compass and Crest

Crest is the closest project to Compass: also open source, also an Arc replacement, also with spaces, Peek, small windows, splits and an MCP server.

Crest has, Compass doesn't:

  • A WebKit engine per site: full-quality DRM video on those pages.
  • Built-in ad blocking and on-device page translation.
  • A translucent sidebar.
  • Import from a few more browsers (Opera, Comet and others).
  • Private Spaces locked with Face ID or Touch ID, and Sub-Spaces.
  • An App Store release for iPhone and iPad, and a Homebrew cask.

Compass has, Crest doesn't:

  • Windows, Linux and Android.
  • Account-free, end-to-end encrypted, self-hostable sync (Crest uses iCloud).
  • A Claude chat panel in the browser (Crest has only the MCP server).
  • Chrome extensions on the phone.
  • Live folders, call picture-in-picture, send tab to phone, favorites shared across all spaces.
