Boosts

  

  
A Boost changes how a site looks or works: CSS and JavaScript that Compass Browser adds to every page of that site. Make one in Compass (Settings → Boosts, or “Boost this site” in the command bar), or ask Claude to make one. Share a Boost from its Share button, and whoever opens the link in Compass can add it.

  
A shared Boost travels inside its link, after the #, which browsers never send to a server: this page reads it on your device.
